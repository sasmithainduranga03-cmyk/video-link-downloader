Good to know
This app runs in your browser and has no API key or custom downloader server. Some sites block previews or downloads, and a normal webpage link is not the same as a direct video file link. It does not bypass logins, paywalls, DRM, or platform restrictions. For videos on YouTube or other platforms, use the platform's official download/offline feature when available.
Your link is handled by your browser. The app shell can open offline, but loading and downloading videos requires an internet connection.
